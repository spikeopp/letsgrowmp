Murachh Jan Jaankari

Panchayat se lekar Collector tak: kaun adhikari kya karta hai, paisa kahan se aata hai, uska hisaab kahan dikhta hai, aur gadbadi dikhe to kya karein. Sab kuchh sarkari public records par based.

Kuchh bhi poochhiye

Murachh, Pawai ya Panna ke baare mein sawaal likhiye: kis scheme ka paisa kahan dekhein, kaun sa adhikari zimmedar hai, RTI mein kya maangein. Jawab Claude dega.

Is view mein Claude se sawaal poochhne ki suvidha uplabdh nahi hai. Baaki saare sections poori tarah kaam karte hain.

Claude ke paas sarkari websites ka live data nahi hai. Woh batayega ki data kahan aur kaise milega. Kisi bhi naam ya rakam ko official portal par khud verify karein.

Sarkar ka dhaancha, Murachh se Bhopal tak

Gaon ke kaam do raaston se chalte hain. Vikas aur schemes Panchayati Raj ke raaste aati hain. Zameen, certificate aur kanoon-vyavastha Revenue ke raaste.

Pehla kadam: Murachh khud ek Gram Panchayat hai ya kisi doosri GP ka hissa, yeh lgdirectory.gov.in par "Village" search karke confirm karein. Saare portals mein isi GP ka naam chunna hoga.

Red flag checklist

Portal ka data aur zameen ki asliyat milaayein. Jo gadbadi mile use tick karein. Ek red flag apne aap mein bhrashtachar ka saboot nahi hai, par yeh batata hai ki RTI ya shikayat kahan karni hai. Aapke ticks sirf is browser mein save hote hain.

Kisi ka naam lekar public mein (WhatsApp, Facebook) aarop na lagayein. Pehle documents (RTI jawab, portal screenshot, photo) ikattha karein aur shikayat sahi jagah karein. Bina saboot ke aarop par aap par maanhani ka case ho sakta hai.
Mile red flags: 0

RTI application banayein

Form bharein. Neeche Hindi mein taiyar application ban jayegi. Use copy karke saade kagaz par likhein ya print karwayein, ya rtionline.mp.gov.in par online daalein.

Kya jaankari chahiye
Zaroori baatein: MP mein fees ₹10 hai (BPL ke liye maaf). Photocopy ₹2 prati page. Application ki receiving zaroor lein. Jawab 30 din mein dena hota hai. Jawab na mile ya adhoora ho to 30 din ke andar Pratham Apeeliya Adhikari ko appeal karein, phir MP Rajya Suchna Aayog mein doosri appeal.

Shikayat kahan aur kis kram mein

Neeche wala kram aam taur par sabse tez nateeja deta hai. Har kadam par likhit shikayat dein aur uski receiving ya complaint number sambhaal kar rakhein.

  1. Gram Sabha mein likhit sawaal

    Sawaal likh kar dein aur use minutes mein darj karwayein. Yahi aage ka saboot banta hai.

  2. CEO Janpad Panchayat Pawai

    GP ke kaam, MGNREGA, sachiv ke khilaaf shikayat. Avedan ki doosri copy par receiving lein.

  3. CM Helpline 181

    Phone 181 ya online. Shikayat L1 se L4 tak apne aap upar ke adhikari ke paas jaati hai. Bina santushti ke band na hone dein.

    cmhelpline.mp.gov.in

  4. Jan Sunwai, har Mangalwar

    Collector karyalay Panna aur Janpad star par. Documents ke saath seedhe adhikari ke saamne pesh karein.

  5. MGNREGA Lokpal (Ombudsman)

    Har zile mein MGNREGA shikayaton ke liye alag Lokpal hota hai. Naam aur pata nrega.nic.in par zile ke page par milta hai.

  6. Rishwat maangi gayi? Lokayukta police

    Panna, Sagar sambhag mein aata hai. Rishwat maangne par Lokayukta trap karwa sakta hai. Paisa dene se pehle hi sampark karein.

    mplokayukta.nic.in

  7. Badi aarthik gadbadi: EOW

    Lakhon ke fund ka galat istemal, farzi bill ya farzi labharthi ke sangathit ghotale ke liye.

    eow.mp.gov.in

  8. Kendra sarkar: CPGRAMS

    Kendriya schemes (PMAY-G, MGNREGA, JJM) ki shikayat seedhe mantralaya tak.

    pgportal.gov.in
Yeh portal sirf sarkari public sources ki taraf rasta dikhata hai. Isme di gayi jaankari kanooni salah nahi hai. Adhikariyon ke naam aur scheme ke niyam badalte rehte hain, isliye har baat official portal par verify karein.

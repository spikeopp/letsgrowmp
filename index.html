How government is organised, from Murachh to Bhopal

Village affairs run along two lines. Development work and welfare schemes come through the Panchayati Raj line. Land, certificates and law and order come through the Revenue line.

First step: check whether Murachh is its own Gram Panchayat or part of a neighbouring one. Search the village on lgdirectory.gov.in. You will need that Gram Panchayat's name on every portal below.

Where to complain, and in what order

This order usually gets the fastest result. Complain in writing at every step and keep the receipt or complaint number.

  1. Written question at the Gram Sabha

    Submit your question in writing and have it recorded in the minutes. This becomes evidence for later steps.

  2. CEO, Janpad Panchayat Pawai

    For complaints about Panchayat works, MGNREGA or the Secretary. Get a receipt stamped on your copy.

  3. CM Helpline 181

    Call 181 or file online. Complaints escalate automatically from L1 to L4 officials. Don't let it be closed unless you're satisfied.

    cmhelpline.mp.gov.in

  4. Jan Sunwai, every Tuesday

    Held at the Collector's office in Panna and at Janpad level. Present your documents directly to the officials.

  5. MGNREGA Ombudsman (Lokpal)

    Every district has an Ombudsman for MGNREGA complaints. The name and address are on the district page at nrega.nic.in.

  6. Asked for a bribe? Lokayukta police

    Panna falls under the Sagar division. The Lokayukta can set up a trap when someone demands a bribe. Contact them before paying anything.

    mplokayukta.nic.in

  7. Large-scale fraud: EOW

    For misuse of lakhs in funds, fake bills, or organised fake-beneficiary scams.

    eow.mp.gov.in

  8. Central government: CPGRAMS

    Complaints about central schemes (PMAY-G, MGNREGA, JJM) go directly to the ministry.

    pgportal.gov.in
